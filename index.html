ERP-Ready file exports

Process client files into clean accounting exports, or link approvals to auto-create SAP records.

01

Create or Import

Type one sales invoice, upload batch files (Excel, CSV, JSON), push directly from your ERP, or let a document status create it.

02

BIR Compliance Check

PIMS automatically validates Buyer TIN, line items, and VAT computations against official BIR rules to prevent rejections.

03

Authorize & Transmit

Route invoices through internal sign-off chains if required, then transmit approved records directly to the BIR EIS portal.

04

Monitor BIR Status

Track invoice progression in real time from Sent to BIR to Accepted, backed by full audit trail history.

01

Template Setup

Assign customized templates matching each client's target layout—configuring header/line fields, import logic, and expected export formats for SAP or other ERPs.

02

File Import

Preview and import Excel or CSV files directly, or enable the Documents link to let a workflow approval status auto-create the transaction record.

03

Validate & Resolve

Automatically check required fields and custom value rules. Resolve missing parameters directly in PIMS to flag the record as Processed & Ready.

04

Export to ERP

Download the finalized file in your exact template layout, fully formatted for your accounting team to upload directly into SAP.
